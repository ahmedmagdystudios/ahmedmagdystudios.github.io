CORE ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEMS

First Person Runner is designed around continuous momentum mechanics. Built from the ground up in C++ with custom shaders to deliver buttery-smooth responsive gameplay.

  • Sweep-and-Prune AABB Collision Detection
